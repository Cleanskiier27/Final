## Phase 4.3: ISRU Processing (Months 6-24)

### Water Extraction and Purification

**MPA AI Water Processing**:

**Thermal Extraction Process**:
1. **Heating**: Regolith heated to 100-200°C
2. **Sublimation**: Ice converts to water vapor
3. **Collection**: Vapor condensed on cold surface
4. **Purification**: Filtration + UV sterilization
5. **Storage**: Cryogenic tanks (-20°C)

**System Specifications**:
- Capacity: 200 kg water per day (from 2,500 kg regolith)
- Power: 5 kW (heating dominates)
- Recovery efficiency: 95%
- Purity: 99.9% (meets life support standards)
- Output: 6,000 kg water per month

**Water Allocation** (AI-Managed):
```
Life support: 1,200 kg/month (6 crew)
Oxygen production: 3,000 kg/month (electrolysis)
Agriculture: 1,000 kg/month (greenhouse)
Reserve buildup: 800 kg/month
Total: 6,000 kg/month
```

### Oxygen Production

**Electrolysis Process** (AI-Controlled):

**Water Electrolysis**:
- Input: 3,000 kg water per month
- Output: 2,667 kg oxygen + 333 kg hydrogen
- Efficiency: 75% (energy)
- Power: 4.5 kW continuous (3,240 kWh/month)

**Oxygen Uses**:

```
Life support: 450 kg/month (6 crew)

```

**Hydrogen Byproduct Uses**:
- Fuel cells: Power generation during lunar night
- Propellant: Liquid hydrogen for upper stages
- Chemical reduction: Metal extraction from regolith
- Reserve: Stored for future expansion

### Metal Extraction

**MPA AI Metal Processing**:

**Molten Regolith Electrolysis (MRE)**:
1. **Melting**: Regolith heated to 1,600°C
2. **Electrolysis**: Electric current separates oxygen and metals
3. **Collection**: Molten metal pooled at cathode
4. **Casting**: Metal cast into ingots or parts
5. **Oxygen Capture**: Released oxygen collected as byproduct

**Production Rates**:
- Aluminum: 100 kg per month (primary metal)
- Iron: 80 kg per month
- Titanium: 30 kg per month
- Silicon: 50 kg per month (semiconductors, solar cells)
- Total: 260 kg metals per month

**Metal Applications**:
- Construction: Structural elements, frames
- Repair: Spare parts, replacement components
- Manufacturing: 3D printing feedstock
- Trade: Export to Earth orbit (future)

### Construction Material Production

**Regolith Sintering** (AI-Optimized):

**Process**:
1. **Compaction**: Regolith pressed into molds
2. **Sintering**: Heated to 1,100°C (particle fusion)
3. **Cooling**: Slow cooling prevents cracking
4. **Quality Check**: AI vision inspection

**Output**:
- Bricks: 500 units per month (10 kg each = 5,000 kg)
- Paving: 50 m² per month (landing pads, roads)
- Shielding: Radiation protection panels
- Quality: Compressive strength 30-50 MPa

**Applications**:
- Habitat expansion: Additional modules
- Radiation shielding: Improved crew protection
- Infrastructure: Roads, landing pads, foundations
- Excavation support: Tunnel walls, shaft lining

- Power: 200-500W average (from base supply)

**Waste Streams Processed**:

**1. Plastic Waste** (30 kg/month):
- Sources: Packaging, containers, films
- Process: Pyrolysis (thermal decomposition)
- Products: Fuel oils (15 kg), carbon black (10 kg), gas (5 kg)
- Uses: Fuel for heating, 3D printing feedstock, gas recovery

**2. Metal Waste** (20 kg/month):
- Sources: Food cans, structural offcuts, damaged parts
- Process: Melting and recast
- Products: Aluminum (12 kg), steel (8 kg)
- Uses: Repair materials, construction, manufacturing

**3. Organic Waste** (50 kg/month):
- Sources: Food scraps, packaging, human waste (solids)
- Process: Composting + biogas generation
- Products: Compost (30 kg), biogas (15 kg), liquids (5 kg)
- Uses: Greenhouse soil amendment, energy, water recovery

**4. Electronic Waste** (5 kg/month):
- Sources: Failed components, obsolete equipment
- Process: Component disassembly + material recovery
- Products: Precious metals (0.5 kg), plastics (2 kg), other (2.5 kg)
- Uses: Repair parts, recycled materials

**5. Glass and Ceramics** (10 kg/month):
- Sources: Broken containers, damaged panels
- Process: Grinding + re-melting
- Products: Glass beads (8 kg), aggregate (2 kg)
- Uses: Construction filler, 3D printing

## Risk Management

### Top Risks and Mitigations

**Risk 1: Ice Deposit Depletion**
- Probability: 10% (over 10 years)
- Impact: High (water shortage)
- Mitigation: Multiple mining sites, water conservation, backup from Earth
- AI Response: Reserve monitoring, alternative site prospecting

**Risk 2: Equipment Failure in Mining**
- Probability: 20%
- Impact: Medium (production delay)
- Mitigation: Redundant robots, spare parts, repair capability
- AI Response: Predictive maintenance, task reallocation

**Risk 3: Processing Contamination**
- Probability: 5%
- Impact: Medium (product quality)
- Mitigation: Quality control, filtration, reprocessing
- AI Response: Contamination detection, batch rejection, process adjustment

**Risk 4: NLRS Malfunction**
- Probability: 8%
- Impact: Low (waste accumulation)
- Mitigation: Manual sorting backup, Earth disposal
- AI Response: Diagnostic analysis, component replacement

**Risk 5: Energy Shortage**
- Probability: 12% (lunar night)
- Impact: High (production halt)
- Mitigation: Battery storage, process scheduling, load shedding
- AI Response: Power optimization, non-critical shutdown
